🍪 Gingerbread Recipe

A warm and cozy gingerbread cookie recipe perfect for Santa Claus Lane.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter (softened)
  • 1/2 cup molasses
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  • Cream butter and sugar together.
  • Add molasses and egg; mix well.
  • Stir in dry ingredients until dough forms.
  • Chill dough for 1 hour.
  • Roll out and cut shapes.
  • Bake at 350°F for 8–10 minutes.
