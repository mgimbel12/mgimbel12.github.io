Jack Skellington Candy Apples

White-chocolate coated apples with Jack’s stitched grin—perfect bridge between Halloween and Christmas.

Ingredients

  • 6 Granny Smith or Honeycrisp apples
  • 2 cups white chocolate or vanilla candy melts
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil (optional)
  • Black candy melts or edible black marker
  • 6 wooden sticks

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the apples thoroughly.
  2. Insert wooden sticks firmly into the tops.
  3. Melt white chocolate with coconut oil until smooth.
  4. Dip each apple and let excess drip off.
  5. Place apples on parchment paper to set.
  6. Draw Jack’s eyes, nose, and stitched smile.
  7. Chill for 10 minutes before serving.

Art Concept

Present the apples on a black-and-white striped tray (like Jack’s suit) with neon purple and teal accents to echo Monster High’s bold style.

Monster High Skullette Sugar Cookies

Neon sugar cookies shaped like skulls with pink bows and bold black outlines.

Ingredients

  • Sugar cookie dough
  • Royal icing (white, black, pink, teal, purple)
  • Skull and bow cookie cutters

Steps

  1. Roll out dough and cut skull and bow shapes.
  2. Bake according to recipe and cool completely.
  3. Flood cookies with white icing.
  4. Add neon backgrounds and pink bows.
  5. Outline details with black icing or edible marker.

Art Concept

Use a neon gradient backdrop and comic-style speech bubbles like “Creepmas!” and “Ghoul-tastic!” for your cookie display photos.

Oogie Boogie Bug Cups

Neon-green pudding cups topped with gummy worms and candy bugs—gross-cute and perfect for a Monster High party.

Ingredients

  • Instant vanilla pudding
  • Green food coloring
  • Gummy worms and candy bugs
  • Whipped cream (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare pudding according to package directions.
  2. Tint pudding neon green.
  3. Spoon into clear cups.
  4. Top with gummy worms and candy bugs.
  5. Add whipped cream for extra drama.

Art Concept

Place cups on a dice-shaped stand (Oogie’s casino vibe) with patchwork fabric underneath to nod to Sally.
