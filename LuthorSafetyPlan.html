Strategic Mental Health Protocol

SYSTEM STATUS: MONITORING

1. Threat Assessment (Feelings & Thoughts)

  • Level Green: Mild stress, tired, but still functioning. Use basic coping tools.
  • Level Yellow: Overwhelmed, racing thoughts, irritability, withdrawing from people.
  • Level Red: Thoughts of self-harm, feeling hopeless, “I don’t want to be here.”
  • Immediate Protocol: At Level Red → contact crisis lines or emergency services right away.

2. Contingency Plan (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Pause. Sit down, feet on the floor, take 10 slow breaths.
  • Step 2: Activate calming video feed (below) and follow the breathing or grounding instructions.
  • Step 3: Text or call one safe person and say, “I’m having a hard time and need support.”
  • Step 4: If thoughts escalate or feel unsafe → call 911 or 988 immediately.

3. Allies & Safe Network

  • Inner Circle: List 2–3 trusted people (family, friends, church members) with phone numbers.
  • Professional: Therapist, doctor, counselor, or spiritual leader.
  • Crisis Allies: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line (text HOME to 741741).

4. Lex-Level Prevention Strategy

  • Daily Scan: Morning check-in: “What level am I today—Green, Yellow, or Red?”
  • Environment: Reduce noise, clutter, and toxic conversations; create one calm corner.
  • Data Log: Journal patterns: sleep, stress, triggers, and what helps.
  • Upgrade: Adjust routines based on what your “data” shows—more rest, more support, fewer stressors.

This plan borrows Lex’s strategic mindset—but turns it toward protection, healing, and long-term stability instead of control.

Calm Protocol Checklist
  • 1. Choose a Video: Pick one that matches your current energy—breathing, grounding, or body scan.
  • 2. Commit 10 Minutes: Treat it like a mission: no distractions, phone on silent, lights softer.
  • 3. Notice the Shift: After the video, rate your stress from 1–10 and write down what changed.
  • 4. Escalation Rule: If stress stays high or thoughts feel unsafe → contact crisis support immediately.
