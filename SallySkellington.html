Midnight Keys in Halloween Town

Original Halloween piano-style lyrics & reading
In the crooked moonlight, where the shadows twist and sway, A piano waits in silence for the ghosts that lost their way. Silver keys like spiderwebs, black keys deep as graves, Every note awakens whispers in the alleyways of knaves. Lanterns flicker orange, casting pumpkins’ jagged grins, Children’s laughter turns to echoes as the midnight song begins. Skeletons start waltzing to the trembling minor chords, While witches hum in harmony on broomstick overlords. The melody is fragile, like a cobweb in the wind, Yet thunder rolls beneath it where the nightmares all have sinned. A ghostly hand is playing—no one’s sitting on the bench, Still every chord is dripping with a sweet, enchanting stench. Then softly, like a promise that the dawn will never come, The final notes fall quiet on the hollow, haunted drum. In Halloween Town’s silence, where the dark and dream align, The piano keeps its secrets till the next bewitched chime.
