Life Situations & Mental Health

Different life situations can shape how safe, supported, and hopeful someone feels.

Family Conflict

Ongoing arguments, criticism, or lack of emotional warmth at home can increase anxiety, depression, and feelings of isolation.

Financial Stress

Worrying about housing, food, or bills can lead to chronic stress, sleep problems, and difficulty concentrating.

School & Work Pressure

Bullying, unrealistic expectations, or discrimination can harm self‑esteem and make it hard to feel safe or valued.

Social Isolation

Having few supportive relationships can make it harder to cope with stress and can increase feelings of hopelessness.

Abuse & Neglect

Abuse and neglect are serious harms. No one deserves to be treated this way.

  • Physical abuse: Being hit, pushed, or hurt on purpose. This can cause injuries and long‑term fear.
  • Emotional abuse: Constant insults, threats, humiliation, or control that damage self‑worth.
  • Sexual abuse: Any sexual contact or behavior without clear, freely given consent.
  • Neglect: Not having basic needs met—like food, safety, medical care, or emotional support.

If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services. In the United States, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1‑800‑799‑7233 and the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline is 1‑800‑422‑4453.
