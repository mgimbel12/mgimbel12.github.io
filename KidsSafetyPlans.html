Kids Safety Plans

Fun themes, serious safety.

Halloween Tricks or Treats Safety Plan

  • Costumes: Use bright colors or glow sticks so kids are easy to see.
  • Street safety: Cross at corners, look left–right–left, and walk (no running).
  • Candy check: An adult checks all treats before eating.
  • Buddy system: Kids stay with a trusted adult or group at all times.

Hello Kitty Safety Plan

  • Kindness rules: Use gentle words, share toys, and ask before hugging.
  • Safe spaces: Know which adults and rooms are “safe helpers.”
  • Feelings check-in: Kids can tell a grown‑up if something feels “not okay.”
  • Contact info: Practice saying name, parent’s name, and phone number.

Monster High Safety Plan

  • Be yourself: Celebrate differences, no bullying or teasing allowed.
  • Online safety: Never share full name, address, or school online.
  • Drama rules: Walk away from fights, find a teacher or trusted adult.
  • Emergency words: Teach kids to say “I need help” clearly and loudly.

Tulips Yellow Safety Plan

  • Outdoor play: Stay where adults can see you; no playing near streets.
  • Sun safety: Wear hats, sunscreen, and drink water often.
  • Nature respect: Don’t pick unknown plants or touch bugs you don’t know.
  • Stay together: Use a “yellow tulip” meeting spot if someone feels lost.

Disneyland Safety Plan

  • Meeting point: Choose a clear landmark as the family meeting spot.
  • Ride rules: Follow height rules, keep hands and feet inside rides.
  • If lost: Kids find a uniformed staff member and stay put.
  • ID help: Use wristbands or cards with parent phone numbers.

Safety first, fun always.
