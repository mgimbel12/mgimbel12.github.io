Safety Concept Checklist

This sheet marks out key safety and accessibility concepts for each design theme. The table uses full sentences in the notes column to explain why a concept is considered safe or needs improvement.

Theme Color Contrast Readability Age-Appropriate Imagery Motion / Flashing Safety Inclusivity Notes
Halloween Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes The Halloween design uses high contrast between orange accents and dark backgrounds, and all text is written in full sentences. Imagery is stylized and friendly, and any motion effects are slow and gentle to avoid startling viewers.
Christmas Yes Yes Yes Needs Care Yes The Christmas design maintains good contrast by pairing red and green with white backgrounds. Text is clear and readable, and imagery is age-appropriate. Light effects must be checked carefully to ensure that they do not flash rapidly or cause discomfort.
Easter Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes The Easter design uses pastel colors with darker text to keep content readable. Instructions and messages are written in full sentences, and all imagery is gentle and suitable for children. Motion is minimal and does not distract from the main content.
Disney Castle Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes The Disney Castle inspired design uses deep blues and soft gradients with clear text overlays. Sparkle effects are subtle, and navigation elements are labeled with full sentences to support understanding. The imagery is magical but still safe and age-appropriate.
Princess Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes The Princess design uses soft colors with sufficient contrast and avoids long paragraphs in decorative script fonts. Messages focus on kindness and courage, and characters can represent diverse backgrounds. Motion is limited to gentle transitions that do not distract or overwhelm.
Queen Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes The Queen design uses bold colors with balanced contrast and a clear visual hierarchy. Text is structured in full sentences and organized into sections that support comprehension. Leadership is presented as a positive and inclusive concept that anyone can aspire to.

Overall, these themes respect safety concepts such as color contrast, readability, age-appropriate imagery, motion safety, and inclusivity. Designers should continue to review each element carefully to ensure that the final experience remains safe and welcoming for all users.
